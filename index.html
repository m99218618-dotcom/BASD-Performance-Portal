About the Department

The Basic and Applied Sciences Department is one of the academic departments within the College of Applied and Health Sciences at A’Sharqiyah University. The department contributes to high-quality education in the basic and applied sciences, supports interdisciplinary teaching across programmes, and promotes applied research, innovation, and community engagement.

The department currently hosts academic programmes in Industrial Chemistry and Mathematics and contributes foundational scientific expertise across the University. Current programmes listed by ASU include:

  • Diploma in Industrial Chemistry
  • Bachelor of Science in Industrial Chemistry
  • Bachelor of Sciences in Mathematics

Through its teaching, research and service activities, the department aims to strengthen scientific thinking, quantitative reasoning, problem solving, innovation, and the application of knowledge to challenges relevant to Oman and the wider region.

Vision

To be a leading department in basic and applied sciences in the region, recognized for excellence in education, research, and innovation.

Mission

To deliver high-quality, future-focused education and applied research in the basic and applied sciences; foster innovation and collaboration; and engage with community and industry to support sustainable development and contribute to national and global progress.

Alignment: The departmental vision and mission above are adapted from A’Sharqiyah University’s current Vision and Mission so that BASD priorities remain directly aligned with the University’s direction.
